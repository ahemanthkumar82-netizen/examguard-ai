## 🚀 How to Use

### **For Students:**
1. Open the student portal link
2. Click "New Student? Register Here"
3. Fill in your details (name, phone, email, college, register number)
4. Create a password
5. Login with your register number and password
6. Click "Start Camera & Begin Exam"
7. Allow camera permissions
8. Take your exam!

### **For Administrators:**
1. Open the admin panel link
2. Login with admin credentials
3. View dashboard statistics
4. Manage students, sessions, and violations
5. Generate reports

## ⚠️ Important Rules

Students must follow these rules during exam:

1. ✅ Only ONE person in camera view
2. ✅ Keep face visible at all times
3. ✅ Look at the screen (not away)
4. ✅ Don't look down for extended periods
5. ✅ Keep head facing camera
6. ✅ Stay awake and alert
7. ✅ Don't block the camera

**Violations will result in warnings or exam termination!**

## 💡 Tips for Best Experience

### Camera Setup:
- Position camera at eye level
- Ensure good lighting (face clearly visible)
- Avoid backlighting (window behind you)
- Stable internet connection
- Quiet environment

### Browser Settings:
- Use latest browser version
- Allow camera permissions
- Enable JavaScript
- Clear cache if issues occur

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working?
- Check browser permissions
- Try different browser
- Restart browser
- Check if camera works in other apps

### Can't Login?
- Check register number spelling
- Verify password is correct
- Try "Forgot Password" (if available)
- Contact administrator

### Page Not Loading?
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private mode
- Try different browser
